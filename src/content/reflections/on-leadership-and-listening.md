---
title: On leadership and listening
date: 2026-03-02
description: A short note on why the best leadership decisions I've made started with shutting up.
tags: [leadership, career]
---

The pattern shows up again and again: the meetings that go well are the ones
where I talk least. Not because I have nothing to add, but because the person
closest to the problem usually already knows the answer — they just need space
to say it out loud.

This is easy to nod along to and hard to actually practice, especially under
deadline pressure when it feels faster to just decide. But the decisions I've
regretted were almost always the ones where I skipped the listening step.
