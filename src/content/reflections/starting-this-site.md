I've wanted a place to put down thoughts that don't fit anywhere else for a while.
Not a blog with a content calendar, not a portfolio optimized for recruiters —
just a spot to think out loud about leadership, training, family, and the rest.

This site is intentionally plain. No heavy framework, no tracking, no comments
section to maintain. Georgia for reading, a flat nav, and a reverse-chronological
list of whatever I've written. If it grows into something more structured later,
it will, but the starting point is just: write things down.
